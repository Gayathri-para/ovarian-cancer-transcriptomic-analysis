# Results

## Ovarian cancer cohort
The thesis reports differential gene-expression analysis on **96 ovarian cancer patient samples**.

The analysis produced:
- Differentially expressed genes
- Volcano-plot visualization
- Hierarchical clustering heatmap
- Transcriptomic patterns across samples

The thesis describes substantial transcriptional dysregulation and sample/gene clustering patterns, supporting further investigation of molecular pathways and candidate biomarkers.

## Pilot studies

### Drosophila ageing
A Day 1 vs Day 60 RNA-seq comparison was used as pilot work. The thesis reports transcriptional changes associated with ageing-related processes.

### Cell-density effects
A sub-confluent vs confluent comparison was used as a second RNA-seq pilot. Reported pathways included nucleosome organization, chromatin assembly, mTOR signalling and spliceosome-related processes.

## Limitations
The thesis notes large-data handling, computational limitations and pipeline troubleshooting as practical challenges.

Only validated outputs from the original work should be added to this repository.
